Restore point documentation and standard operating procedures.

## Function and Purpose
My Auction Portal is a live production environment owned and operated by The Auction Department Limited. 

**Primary Function**: To provide a comprehensive, white-labeled virtual auction department for partner Estate Agencies. It facilitates the management of business profiles, staff personnel, and the clinical upload of property, seller, and solicitor data for auction disposal.

**Core Purpose**: To empower Estate Agents to offer professional auction services to their clients without the need for internal investment in specialist staff or resources. The Auction Department Limited (TAD) handles the execution and sale, while the portal serves as the definitive audit trail and intelligence relay.
