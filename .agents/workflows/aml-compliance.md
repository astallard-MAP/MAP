# AML Compliance Workflow (TAD-AML-001)

This workflow defines the forensic lifecycle of an AML case at The Auction Department Limited, ensuring strict adherence to the **Money Laundering Regulations 2017**, RICS rules, and HMRC Supervision.

## Stage 1: Identification & Registration
1.  **Onboarding**: New vendor instruction or bidder registration.
2.  **Case Creation**: System assigns a unique **AML-2026-XXXXX** identifier.
3.  **CDD Requirements**: 
    - **Individuals**: Obtain List A (Identity) and List B (Address) documents.
    - **Entities**: Verify legal existence (Companies House), identify Beneficial Owners (>25%).
4.  **Sanctions/PEP Screening**: Mandatory check against OFSI/UN lists via Frank AI.

## Stage 2: Risk Assessment & AI Audit
1.  **Risk Rating**: Internal assignment of **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** risk.
2.  **Frank AI Analysis**: 
    - AI scans for "Red Flags" (e.g., unusual urgency, cash deposits >£2k, third-party payments).
    - AI confirms if **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)** is mandatory (PEPs, High-Risk Jurisdictions).
3.  **Checklist Execution**: Administrator follows the AI-generated procedural checklist.

## Stage 3: Investigation & MLRO Review
1.  **Internal SAR**: Staff members report suspicions via the **Internal SAR Form**.
2.  **MLRO Audit**: Nominated officer reviews the case file, evidence, and AI recommendations.
3.  **Defence Against Money Laundering (DAML)**: If required, the MLRO requests consent from the NCA before proceeding.
4.  **Record Keeping**: All evidence and decisions stored for **5 years** minimum.

## Stage 4: Reporting & Closure
1.  **External SAR**: MLRO submits a report to the **National Crime Agency (NCA)** via the SARs portal if suspicion persists.
2.  **Tipping-Off Control**: Strict confidentiality maintained to avoid Section 333A POCA offences.
3.  **Resolution**: Case closed once CDD is complete or the transaction is formally rejected.
